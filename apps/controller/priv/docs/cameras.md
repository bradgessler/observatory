# Cameras

Two kinds of camera, in one place under **Cameras** in the sidebar (and on
Home on a phone):

- **Telescope Camera**: a small USB camera in the telescope's focuser, in
  place of an eyepiece. It's for focusing and for working out where the
  telescope is pointing (plate solving), not for pretty pictures. See
  [Telescope camera](/docs/scope-camera).
- **Observatory Camera**: a webcam pointed at the mount, so you can see it
  move from anywhere: is it about to wrap a cable, did it stop where it
  should. See [Observatory camera](/docs/watch).

Each is found by itself when it's plugged in: **Devices** (under System) says
which cameras were found and which job each is doing. See
[Adding hardware](/docs/devices).

**All Cameras** shows each one's latest picture side by side, its name, and
one line on how it is. Tap a picture for that camera's page; each camera's
**Settings** and **Frames** are pages under it.

Looking at All Cameras keeps the observatory camera's stills coming, as its
own page does. It doesn't switch on the telescope camera's Live View, which
would change the camera for everyone watching; its picture says how old it
is instead, and Live View is one tap away on its page.

## Finding a page

Search finds any page by a bit of its name: **⌘K** (Ctrl-K, or `/`) on a
computer, the magnifying glass at the top of any page on a phone. Opened
with nothing typed, it lists the help for the page you're on, then every
page.

## Driving and looking

What the telescope camera sees is also on the keypad (Axis Strips), beside
the strips: the viewfinder. Tap it for the camera's own page. There, **Move
the Scope** has Nudge's arrows, to center what's in the picture without
leaving it.
