# Nudge

Tap an arrow; the mount moves exactly one step — 1′, 5′, 30′ or 2° — and
stops. Nothing to hold, nothing to time.

## Why it exists

Centering a star in an eyepiece with a held button is a game of reflexes. A
known step is deterministic: tap, look, tap. It is also the safest surface to
hand to someone: no step is bigger than 2°.

## Notes

* Directions are sky directions (E/W turn the RA axis, toward/away the Dec
  axis), through whatever pointing model is active.
* Each tap is a slew of exactly one step, at the mount's own speed with its
  own ramps.
* STOP is in the page's header; a step cannot run away.
