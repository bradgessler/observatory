# Position

Type where each axis should be, in degrees from home, and go there — or go
home.

## Why it exists

The put-it-back tool. After an evening of wandering, "0°, 0°" is counterweight
down, tube at the pole, ready to be capped and carried. It is also the
plainest way to move an exact amount when debugging.

## Notes

* Degrees are the mount's own axis angles, not sky coordinates.
* Home must have been set (Setup, or the first step of Line Up); that is
  also what arms the soft limits.
